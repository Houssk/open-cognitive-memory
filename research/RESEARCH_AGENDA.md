# Research Agenda

## Research axes

1. Human cognitive organization and memory.
2. Associative memory and free association.
3. Knowledge representation and temporal cognitive graphs.
4. Memory architectures for AI agents.
5. Continual learning, consolidation, and forgetting.
6. Human–AI comparison and evaluation methodology.

## Initial hypotheses

**H1 — Association signal:** structured free-association observations contain information about an individual's knowledge organization beyond basic demographic or topical questionnaires.

**H2 — Longitudinal change:** repeated observations can detect meaningful changes in association and graph structure, while accounting for context and measurement noise.

**H3 — Consolidation utility:** explicit memory consolidation can improve one or more outcomes (retrieval quality, consistency, latency, or storage cost) relative to non-consolidating baselines.

**H4 — Human–LLM differences:** human participants and language models produce measurably different association distributions under matched prompts and controlled conditions.

**H5 — Provenance benefit:** preserving source and transformation provenance improves confidence calibration, auditability, or reliability in downstream retrieval.

**H6 — Continual update trade-offs:** memory update strategies exhibit measurable trade-offs between retention of prior knowledge, incorporation of new information, interference, and resource use.

## Experiment design requirements

Every experiment should specify:
- Question and hypothesis.
- Target population or model family.
- Sampling and recruitment procedure.
- Data collection protocol.
- Variables and operational definitions.
- Baselines and controls.
- Metrics and statistical analysis plan.
- Sample-size rationale where applicable.
- Privacy, consent, and ethics review requirements.
- Reproducibility package and limitations.

## Initial experiment portfolio

- E01: Free association protocol and test–retest reliability.
- E02: Longitudinal cognitive graph evolution.
- E03: Consolidation strategy comparison.
- E04: Human vs LLM association patterns.
- E05: Knowledge structuring and provenance.
- E06: Associative retrieval benchmark.
- E07: Forgetting, decay, and memory selection.
- E08: Continual learning and interference.

The experiment names are planning identifiers, not completed or preregistered studies.
