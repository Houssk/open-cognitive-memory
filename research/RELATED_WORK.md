## Review template

For each paper or system:
- Citation and URL/DOI.
- Research domain and question.
- Construct definitions.
- Method, data, and sample.
- Main findings.
- Limitations and threats to validity.
- Reproducibility status.
- Relevance to OCM.
- Open questions and competing explanations.
